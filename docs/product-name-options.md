# Bloom Memories — Product name options

## Confirmed working name
**Bloom Memories** — the selected name for this portfolio concept. It evokes memories growing over time and works for travel, friendship, and family keepsakes.

## 10 similar concept names (reference only)
1. **My Bloom Memories** — more personal and intimate.
2. **Bloom Memory Book** — directly communicates a keepsake album.
3. **Bloom Postcard Book** — emphasizes the postcard format.
4. **Little Bloom Memories** — warm and giftable.
5. **Memories in Bloom** — poetic, suitable for a brand tagline.
6. **Bloom Keepsakes** — broader room for future keepsake products.
7. **Petal Memory Book** — natural and gentle.
8. **Postcards in Bloom** — more editorial and story-led.
9. **Bloom Journey Album** — foregrounds travel memories.
10. **Our Blooming Days** — focuses on shared everyday moments.

## Naming note
These are brainstorming candidates, not cleared names. Bloom Memories is used here as an unofficial portfolio concept. Before public or commercial use, check trademark rights, domain availability, and any risk of implying official affiliation. The prototype is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo or the Pikmin Bloom development team.
